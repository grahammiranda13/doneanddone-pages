Delete your account

The fastest way is inside the app: open Settings → Delete my account and enter your password. Your account, name, email, device tokens and circle memberships are removed immediately.

If you cannot open the app, send a deletion request below. The person who operates your Done & Done server will remove the account and its data within 7 days and will not use the address for anything else.

Read the privacy policy for what is stored and what remains with your circles after deletion.
