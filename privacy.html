Done & Done

Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Done & Done is a private task messenger. People send task cards to the circles they share instead of chatting. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and how you can remove it.

Short version

Your name, email, tasks and push token are stored on the private server that runs your circles. They are shown only to people in your circles. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not sell data. You can delete your account inside the app.

What the app stores

  • Account details: your name and email address.
  • Sign-in data: your password is stored as a one-way hash by the server; the app never keeps your password.
  • Task content: task titles, notes, due dates, checklist or shopping items, assignments, completions and the small activity events shown in a circle.
  • Circle details: the circle name, its owner and its members.
  • Notification token: only if you turn on mobile notifications, so the server can ask Expo to deliver a “task update” alert. The alert does not include task text.

Why it is stored

The data is used only to sign you in, keep your circles and tasks in sync, and send the notifications you asked for. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

Who can see it

Task and circle data is limited to the members of that circle. The server operator can access the server to run and maintain it. The app does not include third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software. Push alerts are handed to Expo’s push service for delivery; that service receives the notification text and your push token, not your task list.

Where it is stored

The app talks to a self-hosted Appwrite server chosen by the person who runs your circles. Data travels over HTTPS. The server keeps the data until you delete it or the operator removes it.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Delete my account and enter your password. Your account, email, name, device tokens and circle memberships are removed. Tasks you sent stay with their circle but are anonymised, and completed tasks keep their historical completion time. If you were the last member of a circle, that circle and its tasks are deleted. You can also copy your task data as JSON from Settings before deleting your account.

If you cannot open the app, request deletion from outside the app at the account deletion page.

Children

Done & Done is not directed to children under 13, and the app does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the data on your server should go to the person who invited you or who operates your Done & Done server.
